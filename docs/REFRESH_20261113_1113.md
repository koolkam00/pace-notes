## Edition policy

[`analysis/edition-policy-20261113-1113.json`](../analysis/edition-policy-20261113-1113.json) (`fe5707e1…75a2`; `policy_sha256` `6fd848db…22cd`; script `analysis/source_quality.py` `10b6bee6…`), rebuilt from the adapted input by [`policy.py`](evidence/2026-11-14/release-20261113-1113/policy.py), which fails on any difference. The editions it does not count are published once, in `public/data/edition-policy.json` (`c1916125…f024`; R8); manifests carry its digests and counts.

**The rule, frozen in words before the numbers were read.** An edition is **counted** (supplies every pooled statistic and peer comparison) only when all hold: (1) split tier `splits`; (2) tier `stamped`, `stamped_weaker_source` or `partial`; (3) stamped, or partial with finishes with a time between 95% and 105% of the official count (R3: New York 2014 at 94.11% is not counted); (4) it passes the three timing screens with the thresholds of October 16, 2026 (under 5% usable; at least 75% of the quickest quarter against under 50% of the slowest; at least 25% mixed-basis grids), [`edition-screen.csv`](evidence/2026-11-14/release-20261113-1113/edition-screen.csv); (5) it is not a reviewed exception. The 39 decisions of `RULES_MASTER_20261103B` are carried as exceptions (four are needed, since the rule alone would count Boston 2013, Chicago 2005, Madrid 2023 and Auckland 2017), plus Honolulu 2021 to 2025 (`mixed_distance_list`). Review: [`edition-policy-review.json`](evidence/2026-11-14/release-20261113-1113/edition-policy-review.json), [`edition-policy-decisions.json`](evidence/2026-11-14/release-20261113-1113/edition-policy-decisions.json).

**Result: 201 counted** (stamped 162, `stamped_weaker_source` 12, partial 27) in 28 labels; 2,487 listed as not counted: `finish_time_only` 1,926, `unverified` 202, `held_for_review` 163, `invalid_split_grid` 158, `partial_coverage` 12, `unreconciled_hold` 12, `selected_field` 6, `mixed_distance_list` 5, `incomplete_ingestion` 3 ([cohort.json](evidence/2026-11-14/release-20261113-1113/cohort.json)).

**Two levels of use (R4).** A record in an edition that is not counted only because it is `unverified` or `partial` outside the band, with a grid that passes the screens, keeps an **own-record** pacing view with its tier label, and enters no pooled figure: 218,435 finishes in 32 editions. Held, finish-time-only, invalid-grid and reviewed-exception editions give recorded times only. The lookup carries a status per marathon record.

**Weaker-source editions are counted (R5)**; the rate without them and on verified tiers only is printed on `/methodology`.
