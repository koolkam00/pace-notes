The producer feature `valid_splits` population and the site's final analytical population are different definitions (1652: 4,007,503 against 3,871,767; 0934: 3,558,154 against 3,517,336). The former has its own producer sustained-slowdown flags (1652: 1,209,910; 0934: 1,082,894); the current supporting study independently recalculates its own measure from the latter cohort, so those counts must not be substituted. Missing age or recorded gender alone does not exclude a valid finish from the overall cohort.
