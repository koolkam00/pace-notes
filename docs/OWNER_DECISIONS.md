# Owner decisions

Every decision the owner has made about this project, with the owner's words where they were recorded. Quotes are exact, spelling included ("reccomend", "documenatio nand"). Where no quote exists the row says so, and a choice the owner did not make (a specification's default, applied by the build) is labelled **Default** and listed apart, so it is never read as the owner's word. The documents named under Source keep their own copies; this register points to them and does not replace them.

To add a decision: append a row (oldest first), quote the owner exactly and give the date, what it supersedes, what enforces it (a check, a pin, a policy file or a rule in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)) and where it is recorded. When a later decision supersedes an earlier one, fill in both rows; never delete a row. Open questions for the owner are listed in the [project handoff](PROJECT_HANDOFF.md#open-for-the-owner).
