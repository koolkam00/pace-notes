To add a decision: append a row (oldest first), quote the owner exactly and give the date, what it supersedes, what enforces it (a check, a pin, a policy file or a rule in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)) and where it is recorded. When a later decision supersedes an earlier one, fill in both rows; never delete a row. Open questions for the owner are listed in the [project handoff](PROJECT_HANDOFF.md).
