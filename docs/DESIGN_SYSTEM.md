# Design system: race-day paper

The site's look is "race-day paper": warm paper pages, a few night sections for the moments that should feel like a race morning, and charts drawn by hand in SVG or Canvas. Tokens live in `app/globals.css` and story components in `app/story.css`.

> **Redesign in progress (October 17, 2026, branch `feat/redesign`, not yet on main or the live site).** There, `app/globals.css` and `app/story.css` are split by owner into `app/styles/legacy/`, loaded after the new `app/styles/{fonts,tokens,base,layout}.css` and `app/styles/components/` (one sheet for all of them, `app/styles/site.css`). The redesign sets three faces: Fraunces 440 (headings and the reading's sentence), Inter (text, labels, and now kickers and ticks) and Bricolage Grotesque 660 (figures only); JetBrains Mono stays for the legacy rules alone. Evidence badges are a glyph and a word with no hue. The primitives are in `components/ui/` and shown at `/design-system` (noindex). This document is rewritten from the redesign spec when the redesign lands (WP9).
>
> **The shell on `feat/redesign` (WP0-B, October 17, 2026, not yet on main or the live site).** The site reads as eleven chapters in three parts, registered once in `lib/chapters.ts` (pages in their "next" order, readings as nugget ids, "How we know" anchors; checked by `scripts/verify-chapters.cjs`). The header (`components/SiteHeader.tsx`, `app/styles/components/header.css`) is 56 px on phones and 64 px from 900 px; under 1180 px its Menu button opens `components/MenuSheet.tsx`, a bottom sheet under 600 px and a right drawer to 1179 px, with `nav#site-menu` (the five links and the `.nav-more` line, as S1 pins them), the chapters on their dashed course line (`components/ui/ChapterList.tsx`) and Methodology · Privacy. From 1180 px the five links sit in the header and a Chapters button opens a drawer of the chapters alone. The "by Andrew Kam" credit is in the footer's brand line on every page and, since WP1 (October 17, after the critic pass had taken it out), quiet and small under the header's brand at every width. On a page whose root carries `data-reading` (`readingAttrs()`), a reading bar takes the header's place while it is away. Pages in the race end on `components/ChapterEnd.tsx`: the night next card (`NextCard.tsx`, the finish card on `/tools/plan`), the way back, How we know and "Was this useful?". `components/ChapterLink.tsx` carries a goal in the address (`goal=`) along the race. The footer (`components/SiteFooter.tsx`) is the brand line, Methodology, About, Privacy, Request an analysis, Open data and the Motion switch (`role="switch"`). From 1180 px, `components/ChapterRail.tsx` is the chapter rail for pages laid out in `.with-rail`.
>
> **Charts, the replay and the nuggets on `feat/redesign` (WP0-C, merged with the shell on October 17, 2026; not yet on main or the live site).** Chart forms are server-drawn SVG in `components/viz/` (ChartFrame, Readout, Axis, Annotation and the forms the spec lists), coloured from tokens only; `Scrubber.client.tsx` is the one touch control (needle and pick modes, detents, keyboard). The replay draws rank lanes: `ReplayPoster.tsx` is in place at build, and `ReplayStage.client.tsx` loads the canvas code (`RaceReplay.tsx`, its own chunk) and the rows once the replay is within a screen of the viewport; the edition is the one the data picks unless the page names one (the home page still does, until WP1). Every headline number comes from `lib/nuggets/` (`getNugget`, `readingOf`; recounted by `scripts/verify-nuggets.cjs`, which also lists the placeholders area packages still replace). Story next cards show the next page's reading, or the next chapter's lead when the page opens a chapter. The replay description below is main's.

> **Primitives after the October 17, 2026 critic pass (`feat/redesign`, not yet on main or the live site).** Orange means the reading, the reader's ring or the primary button only: the pace ramp's slow side is rose to crimson (below); a picked mark (scatter, waffle) takes a 2 px ink outline, never an orange ring (a dot strip's picked dot is filled and enlarged instead, see the October 18 note); a waffle is small ink tiles (at most 12 px, 2 px gaps, centred) with the read-out tile in signal; a histogram's needle runs from the top of the bin it reads to the baseline, and the headline's tower turns ink while the needle is elsewhere. Every `--fill-quiet` mark carries a `--mark-quiet` edge (`verify-css` check 7). `EffectBand` takes per-°C data and a required unit system and shows the reader's degree (`perDegree`, checked in `verify-units`), on a scale fitted to its intervals. The share bar names its subject above the bar in signal ink on a leader; a segment carries a label inside only where the text holds 4.5:1 in both themes. Make it yours is one inline 48 px row at the top of its beat ("Change" opens the sheet) and never floats. Guess first on a ruler uses the ruler as the control (the You ring is the thumb). The receipt states its baseline once, in its footer, has no ruler, and reads "behind". Values puts the finding second and fades its right edge 24 px. The reading bar is solid paper. The closing stack has no also-in-this-chapter rows. The replay's play button sits in the control row beside the clock, its sentence steps down to `--night-ink-3` (a solid 5.4:1) while the race runs, and the route view takes the course's own shape. `inWords(share, noun)` reads as a phrase in both branches; `stripExtremes` names a dot strip's extremes from courses with three or more editions only.

> **Rings after the October 18, 2026 critic pass (`feat/redesign`, not yet on main or the live site).** An open ring means the reader and nothing else. A comparison the headline is measured against is a small filled dot: `LineProfile` points take `kind: 'compare'` (a 4 px ink dot, class `is-compare`), the ruler's `compare` mark, and Guess first's comparison (filled `--ink-3`). A dot strip's picked dot fills with ink and grows to 14 px (signal while it is the subject), and strips draw only courses with three or more editions, the rest staying in Values, so they need no hollow marks or key. Once a dots guess is revealed, the guess's rings step back to a hairline `--ink-3` ring, so the truth's signal dots lead. A big number's suffix (%, ×, min, "in 25") never renders under 12 px. `LineProfile` labels accept a `gap` for a label that must stand clear of a climbing line.

## Type

| Role | Family | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Fraunces Variable (`--serif`) | Page and chapter titles, with an italic orange emphasis (`<em>`) |
| Body | Inter Variable (`--sans`) | Prose, controls, chart labels |
| Labels | JetBrains Mono Variable (`--mono`) | Eyebrows, kickers, axis ticks, small data labels |
| Numerals | Bricolage Grotesque Variable (`--bib`) | "Bib" numbers, nugget figures, stat tiles |

The fonts are self-hosted through `@fontsource-variable/*`, imported in `app/layout.tsx`. Verifier-loaded components must not import CSS or fonts.

- **Fallbacks.** Each stack names a metric-matched fallback right after its web font, before the same family installed on the device (an installed static Inter draws bold copy at 700, not the web font's 650, and rewrapped the home dek when the web font arrived), with the `@font-face` rules at the top of `app/globals.css`: a system font scaled to the width of the site's copy in the web font (`size-adjust`) and given the web font's ascent, descent and line gap, so text keeps its line breaks when the web font swaps in. Fraunces falls back to Times New Roman Bold (titles at 680 and headings at 600–640 matched separately, italics on their own, all at their display optical sizes; the italic is set at 96.5% of its match, because the italic file arrives after the upright one and a heading's italic phrase is drawn in the fallback in between, and at 100% that gave the race replay story's first section title two lines for that moment), Inter to Arial and Arial Bold, JetBrains Mono to Courier New, Bricolage Grotesque's figures to Arial Bold; Android, which has none of these, gets Noto Serif and Roboto. Card titles set at optical size 72 or below are wider in Fraunces than the display match, so they can gain a line when it arrives. The home title steps down to 2.65rem at 339 px and narrower, where its three lines fill the column in Fraunces and would need four in the fallback.
- After changing a font package, a weight or a title's variation settings, rebuild and run `node scripts/font-fallbacks.cjs` (it needs the Liberation fonts, which share Times New Roman, Arial and Courier New's metrics) and paste its rules; `--check` compares them with `app/globals.css`.

- **Diverging pace ramp** (faster → slower, heatmaps only): `#1D3FD8 #4F79F7 #9DB6FB #EFE8DA #EDA6C1 #C65F9E #8F1449` (`PACE_RAMP`, `paceColour` and `paceText` in `lib/viz/format.ts`). Since October 17, 2026 the slow side runs through rose to a deep crimson instead of through orange, so a slow block never reads as the signal: every warm stop is at OKLab ΔE ≥ 15 from `--signal`, `--night-signal` and the dark theme's signal, and every colour on the warm half at ≥ 14 (`scripts/verify-css.cjs` check 8; ΔE 20 is not reachable for a red-family ramp at the cool side's mirrored lightness without turning violet). The ends pass the dataviz validator on paper (CVD ΔE 24.3, normal 29.8).
- **Temperature ramp**: cool blue → paper → hot red (`tempColour` in `components/story/WeatherStory.tsx`).
- **Pacing-type colours**: `lib/viz/palette.ts`.

## Rules

- **Units.** Miles are the default. Any distance, pace, elevation or temperature in client copy goes through `useUnits` (`Distance`, `Pace`, `Section`, `Temperature`, `TemperatureStep`, `PerDegree` in `components/story/Units.tsx`). Analysis thresholds stay metric. Recorded sections are labelled by their boundaries, never as invented mile splits.
- **Language.** Say "sustained slowdown", never "the wall". Use association language only. Counts are finishes. Story copy contains no release tags. Tool results from data are observed shares of complete finishes, never "your chance".
- **Tools.** Results appear instantly without a submit button and default to a full example (a 4:00 goal). The results region is `aria-live="polite"`. Inputs live in the URL so links can be shared, except birth dates.
- **Motion.** The footer's `MotionToggle` pauses every animated figure for the whole site, and keeps the phone header in place. It is stored per browser, sets `data-motion-off` on `<html>` and feeds `usePrefersReducedMotion`. Content animations that loop (six runners, the ghost race, the two runners) also have their own `.viz-pause` button. The replay has play and pause controls.
- **Charts.**
  - Every SVG has `role="img"` and an `aria-label` stating the finding. Labels stay static; they do not change every frame.
  - Anything shown only on hover also has a keyboard path: a range input (`.heat-slider`), a select, or arrow keys with a roving `tabIndex` (the same-course pairs grid).
  - Repeated controls on one page get distinct accessible names (for example each replay chart's race picker).
  - Tick density adapts to width; nine-section axes switch to boundary labels on narrow screens (`SectionAxis`).
  - Pace axes tick on a round minutes:seconds grid in the units shown, never fewer than the five ticks Recharts drew: the coarsest of 0:01, 0:02, 0:05, 0:10, 0:15, 0:30, 1:00, 2:00, 5:00 and 10:00 that gives five or six (9:12 to 9:50/mi reads 9:00, 9:15 … 10:00), or else the finest within six with ticks added past the values to make five (`paceTicks` in `lib/pace-ticks.ts`). Never convert a kilometre grid, which reads 8:15, 8:48, 9:21/mi. Distance axes of section charts tick at the recorded section ends.
  - Charts never depend on colour alone when a label fits.
- **Server and client.**
  - Story bodies are server components. Each wraps its section in `<StoryData value={{ … }}>` (`components/story/StoryData.tsx`) so a dataset crosses to the browser once; charts read it with `useStoryData(key, given)`. Passing the same object to several client components makes React serialize it twice, which doubled the story pages.
  - Pages pass only what the browser needs: `clientArchetypes` drops the per-minute barcode and `replayChoices` keeps only the replay picker's fields (`lib/insights-server.ts`).
  - Shared constants used by server components must not live in `'use client'` modules (see `lib/viz/palette.ts`).
