# Design system

Rewritten October 18, 2026 from the redesign spec (section 3) and the code on `feat/redesign` after the three lanes were integrated (branch state; not yet on main or the live site). The owner's rule wins every conflict: **simple is beautiful**. Every element must help a runner learn the page's finding or act on it; anything else is deleted, not hidden.

## The page

- **A page opens on its reading.** The crumbs, an H1 that is the finding (one italic accent), a dek only where it adds something (never the H1 restated as a question; the question lives in the title and description), then one figure with its sentence (`components/ui/Reading.tsx`; analyses open on their answer card instead, `components/analysis/Shell.tsx`). No page opens on a form or an empty state. A story and the chapter openers (`/finish-times`, `/slowdown`) set the H1 in `t-display`; hubs and info pages use `t-title`, capped from 900 px at 3.9rem (and on the contents at the width of its column) so the home page's H1 (63 px at 1440) is never the smallest display heading.
- **Beats.** After the reading, at most a few beats, each one finding as its H2 (one clause, at most 12 words: `verify-chapters` rule 9, strict on stories) over one figure and at most two sentences. Beats are separated by whitespace and a hairline, never by boxes.
- **Night is the race and the answer.** Dark surfaces (`.night`) mean the replay, the home and course heroes, the analysis answer, a tool's result card and the next card. Never a form, a caption or a promo.
- **Orange is the reading; a ring is you.** A filled signal mark is the one thing the sentence is about; the reader's own guess, goal or time is an open ring; everything else is ink, quiet grey or a validated data colour. "Slower" is never orange. One accent per screen: only the H1's italic is orange; a beat H2 keeps its italic in ink (`.t-h2 em` in `base.css`, `.course-h2 em` on the course pages, `.tool-nugget-text em` on the goal pages), and the marks under it carry the signal. The layout linter fails two coloured heading accents within one screen (check 9) and a term link inside an H1 (check 10).
- **The closing stack** (`components/ChapterEnd.tsx`): the night next card with the next page's reading (`NextCard.tsx`; the finish card on `/tools/plan`), one row with the way back and "How we know →", then "Was this useful?". One way forward and one way back, and one way to the page's methods per screen (since WP9 round 6, October 20, 2026; repository state on `feat/redesign`, not deployed): where the row carries "How we know", the footer's own link to `/methodology` steps aside (`footer.css`, inside `@supports selector(:has(a))`); a story, `/slowdown` and the finish-times summary keep their methods as the last line of their "All numbers" ("How these were measured →", `AllNumbers` `how`) and pass `how={false}`, so their row is the way back alone. Every page in the race ends on it: stories, analyses, `/slowdown`, tools, `/courses` and each course page, `/runners` and `/report`.
- **Budgets** (phone screens of 844 px): home 4.5, story 6, analysis 4, course page 7, tool 5 (its result within 3), hubs 4, `/methodology` with its folds closed 10.

## Type

Three faces, self-hosted from `public/fonts/` (copied from the `@fontsource-variable` packages by `scripts/fonts/copy-fonts.cjs`; `app/styles/fonts.css`; each with a metric-matched fallback from `scripts/font-fallbacks.cjs`):

| Face | Token | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fraunces 440 (wght and opsz axes only) | `--serif` | Headings, the reading's sentence, beat titles; the italic accent (`<em>`, `--signal-ink`) |
| Inter | `--sans` | Text, controls, labels, kickers, ticks and readouts |
| Bricolage Grotesque 660 | `--figure` | The reading figure only (`components/ui/BigNumber.tsx`) |

Code (`<code>`, which only `/methodology` shows) uses the system monospace stack, `--code`; JetBrains Mono and its `--mono` token were dropped with the legacy sheets in WP9 (October 18, 2026). The scale is in `app/styles/tokens.css` and the classes in `base.css`: `.t-display`, `.t-title`, `.t-h2`, `.t-sentence`, `.t-body`, `.t-small`, `.t-label`, `.t-direct`, `.t-kicker`, `.t-tick` and `.t-readout`, with `--t-figure-xl/l/m/s` for big numbers. Nothing on a chart or a control is under 12 px (`verify-css` rule 3). Headings balance their lines (`text-wrap: balance`), so no heading ends on one orphan word.

## Colour

Only `app/styles/tokens.css` holds colour literals (`verify-css` rule 1). Three scopes:

- `:root`, the light page: `--paper` #F5F0E6, `--ink` #15171C (with `--ink-2`, `--ink-3`), hairlines `--rule`/`--rule-strong`, quiet marks `--mark-quiet` (3.5:1) and `--fill-quiet` (always with a `--mark-quiet` edge, `verify-css` rule 7), the reading `--signal` #E5461A (`--signal-ink` for text), the primary button `--accent` #FF5B2E, and a one-hue ordinal ramp `--ordinal-1…4`.
- `.night`, the race and the answer at any theme: the same names take their night values, so a component written with `--ink` or `--signal` reads on either surface.
- **Dark**, the page theme under the reader's system setting (owner decision 2, switched on October 18, 2026): no toggle and nothing stored. The dark values sit twice in `tokens.css`, under `[data-theme="dark"]` and under `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { :root:not([data-theme="light"]) }`, and every component rule written for the attribute has its twin under the media query (`verify-css` rule 9 checks both). Night bands step to `#07090C`; a night card on the dark page takes a hairline ring from `--night-edge`. The viewport `themeColor` is a light and dark pair (`app/layout.tsx`). Share images and OG cards keep their own night palette.

Data colours: the diverging pace ramp (`PACE_RAMP` in `lib/viz/format.ts`, blue to rose to crimson, every warm stop at OKLab ΔE ≥ 15 from every signal, `verify-css` rule 8) and the six pacing-type colours with their night set (`lib/viz/palette.ts`, `typeStyle()`).

## Components

- **Shell:** `SiteHeader` (56 px, 64 px from 900 px; Menu opens `MenuSheet`, from 1180 px the five links and a Chapters drawer), `ReadingBar.client.tsx` (minutes left and a tick per beat), `ChapterRail` from 1180 px (on the stories, analyses and chapter pages; beside it the crumb above the H1 is hidden, so the chapter is named once per screen. In-chain tools, such as the projector, weather match, split check and plan, have no rail: their controls take the width, and the crumb names the chapter), `SiteFooter` (brand line, one row of links, the Motion switch).
- **Chapters:** `lib/chapters.ts` registers eleven chapters in three parts with their pages, readings and "How we know" anchors (`scripts/verify-chapters.cjs`). `ChapterCrumbs` (`components/ui/ChapterHeader.tsx`) is the one chapter line on every page in the race: the marker badge, "Chapter 06 · The finish line" (a chapter after the finish shows its number once, on the badge: "[08] Where you run"), and from 600 px the place ("Page 1 of 2", or a course page's "Courses"), its "·" drawn by CSS so no wrapped line opens on it. `ChapterList.tsx` draws the chapters on their dashed course line in the menu, the home guide and the contents. A chapter may also list a hub (`hub` in `lib/chapters.ts`): a page the chapter names, its menu, rail and contents list, that is not a step of the chain. Chapter 08's is `/courses` (“Every course”, since WP9 round 5): the chain runs from hills and pacing straight to race morning, and `/courses` points back to hills and on to race morning. Page tops share one token, `--page-top` (16 px on a phone, 32 px from 900 px), so a chapter's crumb and H1 land at the same height on every template.
- **Readings and answers:** `Reading`, `BigNumber`, `Receipt` and the analysis `AnswerCard`, the tool result card (`.tool-reading`), `Values` (every chart's table twin, closed by default, its summary "All numbers · N rows" wherever it sits). Since October 19, 2026 a story, `/slowdown` and the finish-times summary gather their tables at the foot in one `AllNumbers` row ("All numbers · N tables", the course pages' pattern; `Values bare` inside it), never one disclosure under each chart. Since October 19, 2026 (WP9 round 5, part B; repository state on `feat/redesign`, not deployed) the night `AnswerCard` holds the answer only: its "All numbers" sits on paper directly under it (`AnswerCard` `values`, `.answer-stack`/`.answer-values`; `WeatherAnalysis` does the same), and `verify-ten-analyses` fails a page with a `Values` disclosure inside a night card or receipt. Make it yours redraws the page's own answer instead of drawing a second chart under the form (`HeroSwap`, `components/analysis/hero-event.ts`): on pacing pattern, where time is gained, finish-time context, hills and pacing, course comparison and race-day weather a selection replaces the card's kicker, chart and "All numbers" in the answer's own grammar (pacing pattern adds the example as a quiet "Every course" line), and the explorer keeps its headline, measured against the example, and one line up to the card ("Drawn in the answer above"). Starting pace, age and pacing, finding improvement and downhill starts keep their own chart under the form, since their answers are a receipt, two lines or two dot grids.
- **Charts** (`components/viz/`, server-drawn SVG coloured from tokens): `ChartFrame`, `Readout`, `Axis`, `Annotation` and the forms `LineProfile`, `Histogram`, `DotStrip`, `DotGrid`, `Waffle`, `ShareBar`, `Ruler`, `Dumbbell`, `Cdf`, `Scatter`, `Waterfall`, `YearStrip`, `ElevationPace` and `TypeGlyph`. `Scrubber.client.tsx` is the one touch control (a needle or a pick, 1:1 with the thumb, detents at the values the headline names, the readout above the plot). A `LineProfile` places its labels clear of the needle where it opens at the reference width (288 px); below it (200% zoom) a gridline, reference or line label that resting needle would run through carries the width class that hides it there, and the readout and "All numbers" keep it (since the WP9 round 5 merge; `lint-layout` check 11). The replay is `ReplayPoster` at build and `ReplayStage.client.tsx` loading the canvas (`RaceReplay.tsx`) near the viewport.
- **Controls:** `Segmented.client.tsx`, `Chips.client.tsx`, `GuessFirst.client.tsx` (at most one per page), `Scrolly.client.tsx` (at most one per story), `MakeItYours.client.tsx` on `Sheet.client.tsx` (the analysis explorer's filters), `components/tools/EvidenceTag.tsx` (the only file that draws evidence badges).
- **Units:** `components/ui/ByUnits.client.tsx` shows the server-drawn miles or kilometres version of a sentence, receipt or chart; `UnitsProvider` holds the switch. Miles are the default.
- **Moved addresses:** `components/MovedPage.tsx` with `LegacyAnalysisLink.tsx` (`/packs`, `/research/personalized`, `/your-race`, `/htw` and the two weather slugs; [SEO](SEO.md)).

## Motion and accessibility

Only transform and opacity animate, and only where motion explains (the replay, a scrub, the reveal of an answer); durations scale with `--motion`, which is 0 under reduced motion and the footer's Motion switch. Every interactive element is at least 44 × 44 px (the layout linter, `scripts/design/lint-layout.cjs`), focus is a 2 px `--focus` ring (`--focus-paper` where a night card's ring is drawn outside it on paper), every chart has a label that states its finding and a Values table, and grey text holds 4.5:1 and marks 3:1 in both themes.

## Copy and numbers

Every figure and directional word comes from a field through `lib/nuggets/` with a guard and a neutral fallback (`scripts/verify-nuggets.cjs` recounts each one independently; no placeholders remain). Insights only on pages; methods, sources and limits live on `/methodology`, and "sustained slowdown" links to `/methodology#sustained-slowdown`. Shares are "of finishes", never "your chance"; the site never says "arithmetic". Number ranges never break at their dash (`joinRanges` in `components/ui/keepTogether.tsx`).

## Checks
