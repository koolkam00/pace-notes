Finish times pile up just before every hour and half-hour. Here is how the pile forms, second by second, and how many finishes slip under the line in the last 2.2K.

Finishes per minute on the clock
THE FIELDEvery column is one minute on the race clock. Together they hold 3.4 million finishes. Half took longer than 4:10.
THE CURVEIf nobody watched the clock, finishes would follow a smooth curve, like this one.
THE TOWERSThey don't. In the minute before every hour and half-hour, a tower pokes above the curve.
THE BUSIEST MINUTEThe busiest minute of all is 3:58, a full minute early: 38,718 finishes.
THE TALLEST TOWERAgainst the curve, the tallest tower is 2:59: 1.73× as many finishes as the curve expects.
