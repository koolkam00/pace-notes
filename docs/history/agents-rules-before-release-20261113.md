4. **Half marathons.** Half figures use the separate pin `analysis/half-release.json` and the reviewed policy file it names (`analysis/half-policy-20261030-1904.json` for `half-dataset-20261030-1904`); the pin names each source's identity as its `key`, which the policy and the reviewed counts are keyed by (`private_tag` is provenance only). Linked figures also bind the adopted marathon release, its runner manifest and the weather evidence. The half results, the producer's person IDs that link half and marathon records (v4; owner decision, 2026-10-31 UTC: "Yes you should adopt v4") and the scripts that built them are the owner's public CC BY 4.0 releases under rule 1 (owner decision, October 30, 2026: "publish everything and full merge"), fetched anonymously by `analysis/download_half.py`.
   Pages still publish aggregates: every shown group holds at least 100 finishes or pairs and a claim across editions rests on at least 3 editions. A verified edition is stamped in the pinned snapshot's own edition metadata and on the policy's reviewed list; the build fails if a stamp the producer withdrew (its `never_stamp` table, cross-checked against the edition metadata of `half-dataset-20261029-2157` the pin names) is stamped again, and a new snapshot or person-ID build shows no half figure until the policy and the link counts are reviewed again. Half-only figures use verified editions; any figure that uses unverified editions carries the "Includes unverified half results" chip; linked figures use set B (v4 person IDs whose person and record confidence are medium or better, `ns_keys ≤ 3` as runner links v1 defined it; records attached without an age or sex never pair, nor do halves outside the link build's coverage or in the policy's link editions) and say "records linked by name". Sustained slowdown stays a marathon metric. After any half rebuild run the local source recount (`npm run verify:half-source`); CI fails on a stale receipt, and the half workflow's `source` job rebuilds from the public assets whenever the pin, policy, builders or recount change. (`verify-half`; [docs/HALF_MARATHON.md](../HALF_MARATHON.md).)

5. **Pins.** Every visible analysis must use the adopted release in analysis/release.json (including supporting study and runner lookup); weather must match through its separate pin. Do not fall back to historical chart files. Preserve historical numerical evidence in Git history and immutable releases. live.json is current compatibility metadata, with no archived figure payloads. Importers have separate ownership and validation contracts. (Each family's verifier in `npm run verify:data` checks its pin.)
6. **Runner context.** Runner context must additionally match the exact runner-manifest hash, timestamp and edition mapping. Refresh/import the lookup before rebuilding context, then use its dedicated importer and full verifier. Same-edition peer groups require 101 finishes (100 others plus the focused record); finish percentiles exclude the focused record, while pace quartiles include it. Weather and supplied-route elevation remain context, never personal-exposure measurements or finish-time adjustments. (`verify-runner-context`; [detail and source conflicts](../RUNNER_CONTEXT_AND_PEERS.md).)
8. **All-finisher views.** All-finisher course/weather/downhill views bind both exact runner and context manifests; rebuild after either changes. Their cross-edition rates average editions equally, while onset and finish-time context pool finishes. Keep those denominators distinct and retain history for personal improvement and other longitudinal questions. (`verify-all-finisher-context`; [docs/ALL_FINISHER_ANALYSES.md](../ALL_FINISHER_ANALYSES.md).)
9. **Record IDs within a release.** The audited `private-export-20260911-1107`, `private-export-20260912-0934`, `htw-db-2026-10-16-1652`, `private-export-20261021-1050` and `master-20261103b` (adapted by `analysis/adapt_master.py`; its marathon `source_record_id` is 1050's id) use canonical raw/feature record IDs with runtime uniqueness, label and full-timing checks. The reviewed `private-export-20261021-0829` is not adopted: its FULL lacks the cross-race identity. September 7 CORE/FULL IDs remain incompatible and require the legacy one-to-one natural match.
10. **Record IDs across releases.** IDs are not stable across releases: 1652 replaced 116,287 0934 IDs (Chicago 2018, Chicago 2019 and Frankfurt 2019 were reloaded), so never carry an ID from one release to another without the runner-manifest mapping (1050 kept every 1652 ID it still holds and changed none of their records). Never infer record alignment from row order or treat cross-race IDs as independently verified people. Since November 5, 2026 (owner decision: "Everywhere"), the marathon's cross-race key is the producer's `person_id` from the adopted master release, under the frozen rule in `analysis/adapt_master.py` (`IDENTITY`), not the v1 `runner_id`; links from a preliminary build (New York 2026's `nyc2026_incremental_preliminary`) are used and marked "Preliminary link" wherever the site shows a runner's linked races (`verify-public-explorer`, `verify-runner-search`).
11. **Edition policy.** Apply the reviewed release-specific edition policy in `analysis/source_quality.py` after timing checks. Keep its exclusion counts and hashes with every result. Invalid, incomplete, held and selected-field editions cannot supply outcomes or earlier benchmarks. Missing age or recorded gender alone does not remove usable timings from the All cohort.
13. **Guards and floors.** Builder floors (such as 100 finishes per group, 3 editions, the half's 10 pairs per edge slice and 20 editions for its women's band medians) decide what is published; page guards decide what is said. When a tail or bin falls under a floor, narrow the published range; never lower a floor or add a compensating check to keep a chart (October 30, 2026: a lowered half floor was found in review and restored). When a number moves and a guard fails, the fallback shows and the guard is re-judged on its merits, never loosened to keep a headline; a share card whose takeaway fails is withdrawn. A fallback states no reading in any slot (heading, dek, hero figure and its key, caption, table reading row, card, the chapter rows on `/` and `/research`, next and night cards, and the `/methodology` sentence about the page), and a figure states a reading only under a guard of its own, else it is a neutral count. A whole figure under a builder floor is left out with its beat. (The family verifiers, `verify-nuggets`; [copy checks](../DESIGN_SYSTEM.md#copy-checks), [half lessons](../HALF_MARATHON.md).)

17. **Words the site never uses.** The owner does not want the word "arithmetic" anywhere on the site. Never "the wall" or "hit the wall" (say "sustained slowdown"), never "your chance" in any form, and never a release tag or its date in visible copy. Enforced by `verify-tools` ("arithmetic" in `app/`, `components/` and `lib/`), `verify-seo` `banned-words` (the three phrases in every built page's text, metadata and JSON-LD), `verify-courses` (the phrases and the marathon release labels, on course pages), `scripts/lib/words.cjs` (`BANNED` and `RELEASE_LABEL`, the one full list of labels, in `verify-charts` and `verify-half`) and `scripts/autopilot-guard.cjs` (the same labels, on autopilot pull requests).
