# Runner context and the race report: earlier records

## Race report: redesign and review notes (October 18, 2026)

From the end of the "Race report" section of `docs/RUNNER_CONTEXT_AND_PEERS.md`.

**Redesign (October 18, 2026; WP6 on branch `rd/l3`, merging into `feat/redesign`; not deployed).** The contracts above hold (the window, groups, badges, link forms, replay masking, events and the privacy rules); the presentation changed. The card is a night "lap screen": the race and its finish, for a record the placement sentence ("Ahead of N in every 100 of the M other eligible finishes in {edition}"), then the lap list (`components/report/LapList.tsx`, shared with the runner view's pacing tab): each mat's split and pace and a bar from the race's own 5–20K pace, with the sustained-slowdown sections highlighted instead of a dashed 25% line. Under it three tiles (the sustained slowdown with its research badge, the pacing type with the data badge, the first 5K), the 20K comparison as a small dumbbell with its values table, and on `/runners` the edition block (`components/report/RecordBlock.tsx`, now loaded on request inside the report) with the late change, weather and climb. The print styles are in `app/styles/pages/report.css` (one Letter page, ink on paper, measured in Chromium on October 18 for a typed report). The image is redrawn as the same night card; its pacing-type line no longer names the cohort or its editions (`label` is "complete finishes"; the cohort is on `/methodology#pacing-types`). The share flow moved into the lazily loaded `lib/report-image.ts` (`shareReport`), which keeps `/report` within its JavaScript budget. `/report` and `/runners` end with the shell's closing stack (`ChapterEnd`, not a tracked section there, so their one `section_viewed` id stays `report`). The runner view no longer uses Recharts: similar runners are a `ShareBar` and a `LineProfile`, weather and terrain plain cells and rows, and a single selected race names its edition, finish and record ID in one line in place of a one-option select. Mats read as 5K … Finish in both unit modes; `verify-runner-search` and `verify-runner-context-ui` now forbid two-decimal miles, and `verify-report` checks the edition block by rendering it on its own.

**Review fixes (October 18, 2026; branch `rd/l3`, branch state, not deployed).** Under the finish, one verdict line ("No sustained slowdown · Gentle fader", the term linked there); the lap bars' scale reaches 15% either side of the 5–20K pace at least, so an even race still draws visible bars, and the bar column is wider. The 20K dumbbell draws this race as the same ink dot in every row, with only the named row's gap in signal; its values table drops the column the dumbbell already gives. The actions are two: "Print or save as PDF" and Share, which opens a sheet (loaded on the first press, rendered in place inside the report's masked region) holding Save image, Share… and Copy link with what the link carries; S3 opens it before those three steps, and, with the profile's analysis link gone, opens the analysis itself with the handoff marker and the race's finish as its goal. In the runner view the late sections of the lap list are marked in ink, not signal (signal keeps one meaning on the page), "Explore pacing across the wider study" is gone (one split-check link stays), a single selected race shows its finish and pace without a count of one or a one-row table, and every climb, descent and net change is in whole feet or metres. The edition block's climb is the sum of the route's 5 km sections, which the course pages now print too: until then the course page printed the smoothed profile's total (Copenhagen 16 ft against the sections' 170 ft), and the two disagreed by up to ten times.

**Second review (October 18, 2026; branch `rd/l3`, branch state, not deployed).** `/runners` and `/report` no longer nest a second `.container` inside the page's, so they share the site's gutter and left edge (the report keeps a 46rem reading width). Each count in a race report names its scope: the 20K comparison's window pools a course's editions ("complete finishes from 3:54:00 to 3:58:59 in Copenhagen, every edition"), and the edition block names its edition where it said "here". The First 5K tile drops its group name, and the comparison heading reads "At 20K, 1:13 ahead of the typical finisher who held pace." In the runner view the pacing tab no longer defines early and late in prose (the lap list's caption says what is highlighted), gives the first 5K only when it parted from the early pace by 1% or more, and steps its lap list aside while the race report, which carries its own, is open below. On a touch screen Share takes the primary look and comes first, Print the outline. The 1080 × 1350 image draws its bars on the lap list's own scale, so the longest reaches the card's right padding. A recorded finish loses its leading zero ("3:44:00", not "03:44:00") in the results and the selection bar, whose chips now snap and fade over 40 px at the edge; the recorded-checkpoint tables keep the source's own formatting.

**Third review (October 18, 2026; branch `rd/l3`, branch state, not deployed).** The report's verdict line under the finish is gone: the tiles say whether there was a sustained slowdown and the pacing type, so the lap screen no longer says both twice (the term now links from the comparison's sentence). From 900 px `/report` sets the tiles and the 20K comparison beside the lap screen; print keeps one column. "Open these splits in the split check" moves into the share sheet, so the page has one onward way, its closing stack. In the runner view a single selected race has no summary card and no "One race. A closer look." heading: its one heading is the race and its finish ("Copenhagen 2022, 3:56:11"), and with two or more races that heading names the focus race. Record numbers leave the visible copy, except in a select where two races would otherwise read the same. The records with limited analysis follow under "N selected records have limited analysis". Each record row is the race and year, its recorded finish and one short tag ("Pacing analysis", "Recorded times only", "Limited: missing checkpoints", "Limited: times out of order" or "Limited analysis"); the recorded name, age, gender and the full reason are in its "Recorded checkpoints" disclosure. The Similar runners tab opens on every eligible finish in the edition, the group the report places the race in, and says the placement once in the report's words ("Ahead of 51 in every 100 of the 8,460 other finishes in this edition", whole numbers down), over a share bar whose segments are labelled "Behind this race" (the lit one, in ink) and "In front of it"; the rank line, the tie count and the placing disclaimer are gone (the report's foot keeps "not an official placing"). A new search that finds no name sets the earlier analysis aside until the selection bar's button brings it back, so it never reads as that search's result. The Conditions tab's warming cell reads "Warmed by 13:00", the observation times, dew point and apparent temperature move into "Hourly readings", and a descent prints with a true minus ("−13 ft").

**Fourth review (October 18, 2026; branch `rd/l3`, branch state, not deployed).** One report screen, one count family and one badge per screen. The report's foot carries the one Pace Notes data badge; the sustained-slowdown tile keeps its Published research badge, and the type tile and the 20K comparison carry none. The pacing type says its share in words over its cohort ("About 3 in 10 of 3,733,382 finishes were gentle faders", the image the same), and a first 5K within half a percent of the 5–20K pace is "Level". On `/runners` the edition block prints with the report but is not shown on screen, since the tabs above say the same (its late line is "+13.8% against its 5–20K pace; similar finishes in this edition: +8.7%", without a second window or count); the report door is one line ("Copenhagen 2022 on one page, to print or share."), and the records with limited analysis are listed under the analysis only when the open profile does not already list them. The pacing tab's lap list lights no rows (lit rows mean a sustained slowdown, and only the report lights them). The Similar runners tab says the placement in the report's exact words ("of the 8,460 other eligible finishes in Copenhagen 2022"), its bar labelled "Behind it" and "In front of it" inside the segments where they fit. The Conditions headings are findings: "54°F near the start, 5°F warmer by 13:00." (whole degrees; the readings to a tenth are in the hourly table) and "Your slowest 5K, 35–40K, is nearly level." (under 10 m net, else its net climb or drop; an even race gets the route's totals). The share sheet's second button reads "Send with another app", and each search result row ends on its arrow alone. Measured on phone (390 × 844) on October 18: the Copenhagen 2022 analysis 5.24 screens (was 5.90), with the report open 6.42 (was 7.40).

**Fifth review (October 18, 2026; branch `rd/l3`, branch state, not deployed).** The placement sentence carries no count ("Ahead of 51 in every 100 other eligible finishes in Copenhagen 2022."), in the report and in the Similar runners tab alike, so it no longer stands beside the 20K comparison's different count (8,460 against 8,641) as if they were one group. The Similar runners bar is a thin data bar, its two segments named under its two ends ("Behind it 51%", "In front of it 49%"), and the late-pace heading is plain: "You slowed 13.8% late; the group’s median slowed 8.7%." A search that finds no name hides the selection bar (the selection is kept, and returns with the next search that finds one), so the bar no longer covers the no-match rows. From 900 px the selected races' analysis reads below the search and its results, centred at the figure measure, with the closing stack following it; the search sticks only beside the results. On `/report` the kicker's parts never break inside ("26.2 MI" stays whole), and the empty page's dek is one sentence. Measured on phone (390 × 844) on October 18: the Copenhagen 2022 analysis 5.24 screens, with the report open 6.42; the no-match page 1.74.

**WP9 round 1 (October 18, 2026; branch `feat/redesign`, committed, not deployed).** The runner view speaks as the report does, in the race's voice, because a name search can find a namesake's record: "Late in this race, the pace was 14% slower than its 5–20K pace.", "This race slowed 14% late; the group’s median slowed 9%.", the first tab "Pacing" and the chart key "This race". Pace changes are whole percent (under half a percent reads as the same pace). The placement drops "eligible": "Ahead of 51 in every 100 other finishes in Copenhagen 2022." From 900 px `/runners` is one centred column with the analysis wider below it, and after "View the selected race" focus moves to the analysis's first heading. The pinned selectors in `verify-runner-context-ui`, `verify-runner-search` and S3 are unchanged; their expected strings follow the new wording.
