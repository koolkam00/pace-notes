# Ten analyses for Pace Notes

## Current all-finisher defaults

Ranks 5 (courses) and 6 (weather) now use the additive [all-finisher context calculation](ALL_FINISHER_ANALYSES.md) by default. The personalized methods and controls documented below for those two ranks describe their retained **earlier-result mode**. The new default selects exact course, age, recorded gender and observed 5–20 km pace bands and does not require a prior result or target. Temperature labels convert to the chosen unit system. No automatic cohort widening applies.

Nine of the ten main questions now offer a default without prior finishes. Rank 9 still measures actual personal improvement and requires an earlier result. The new downhill page and broader archive companions are supplementary, keeping the ten ranks stable.

The site presents ten ranked analyses at `/analyses/{slug}`. On branch `feat/release-1652` (October 16, 2026; not deployed) their shared personalized engine is recalculated from **`htw-db-2026-10-16-1652`**: **3,871,767 eligible finishes**, **1,291,322 with exact age** and **592,241 with a recent earlier-year benchmark**, with 28 cities plus All courses (Melbourne's only edition, 2015, is now excluded as a partial load). Input timestamp: `2026-10-16T20:55:30Z`; calculation timestamp: `2026-10-17T03:49:05Z`. [REFRESH_20261016_1652.md](REFRESH_20261016_1652.md) distinguishes calculation, import and production verification. The previous 0934 refresh (historical) had 3,517,336 eligible finishes, 1,225,873 with exact age and 555,437 benchmarks, with 29 cities plus All courses ([record](REFRESH_20260912_0934.md)).

The original ten-page redesign shipped in [PR #32](https://github.com/koolkam00/htw-live-study/pull/32), main `3e32bf8`, using September 7 data. That is historical deployment evidence, not the current source vintage.
The order prioritizes an understandable runner decision, then strength and availability of the evidence, then additional information beyond the preceding cards. The ranking stays fixed; changing a profile updates results and availability without shuffling priorities.

The subsequent display-unit update defaults to miles and minutes per mile, with a Miles / Kilometres switch and elevation in feet for miles mode. The selection follows links and is shareable as `units=mi|km`; the browser saves the preference. The metric section names in this technical catalog describe source measurements. On the site, a recorded 5 km section becomes 3.11 miles without creating individual-mile splits. Unit selection changes displayed numbers and labels only: the ranked questions, source release, cohorts, counting thresholds and finish durations stay the same. See [display-unit semantics](WEBSITE_ARCHITECTURE.md#display-units). Source-refresh and production verification are recorded separately from this display behavior.

## Primary order and routes

The order, titles, slugs and visible controls are defined in [lib/ten-analyses.ts](../lib/ten-analyses.ts). The home page and `/analyses` list the ten; `AnalysisExplorer` renders one question at a time with profile parameters shared through its links. Nine primary pages use selectors in [lib/personalized.ts](../lib/personalized.ts), fields in [lib/personalized-types.ts](../lib/personalized-types.ts), and [personalized calculations](../analysis/build_personalized.py). Rank 2 now uses the independent [fast-start analysis](FAST_START_ANALYSIS.md), calculated from the same adopted runner data. The earlier personalized opening comparison remains in the twelve-question guide; its under-target shares are not the current rank-2 view.

## Supported controls and counting rules

The profile type has course, age, gender, target and previous time. Weather and terrain are separate data views; there is no shared age × speed × gender × elevation × weather record cube in the published personalized pack. Selecting a warm day must not appear to recalculate a cool-weather pacing profile, and selecting an uphill section must not appear to create a hill-adjusted forecast.

“Speed” has two distinct meanings and needs two labels:

- **Target finish:** a strict counting threshold for ranks 3 and 8; exact neighboring finish windows for rank 4; a centered achieved-time band for ranks 1, 7 and 9 and for rank 10 without history. Whole-minute targets run from 1:30 through 12:00, with exact counts at all 631 thresholds. The engine has 43 presets from 90 through 720 minutes; cells publish only when they meet the method’s sample requirements. Profile bands are 15 minutes wide around the nearest preset, so not every one-minute target adjustment changes those profiles.
- **Earlier recorded best:** an optional 15-minute band of the fastest eligible result in the two strictly earlier calendar years. It is not an exact last marathon time. It changes supported cohorts, especially ranks 5 and 6, but never the checkpoint comparison. Do not silently fill it from the visitor's target. Rank 2's optional history mode instead uses four explicit earlier-best bands (under 3:00, 3:00–3:29:59, 3:30–3:59:59, and 4:00 or longer), plus All. Its default All eligible finishes mode has no earlier-time or speed filter. Both modes use exact cohorts without automatic widening.

Weather supports the existing bands below 10°C, 10–14.9°C, 15–19.9°C and 20°C or warmer only where cells exist. Available bands are determined by the current aggregate and selected cohort. The chart displays those observed bands; it does not generate intermediate temperatures or filter the other nine analyses. At least two bands are needed to claim a cooler/warmer comparison. A one-band result is labeled as coverage, not a cooler/warmer contrast.

Terrain supports a supplied profile for an explicitly selected course. The chart switcher displays net change, supplied gain/loss when available, and the corresponding observed pace profile. There is no elevation slider that generates new runner cohorts, corrected paces or a causal hill adjustment. A separate all-course terrain-class chart already exists in `ext_terrain_pacing_proxy`, but its classes mix different courses and are not the personalized cohort.

The explorer shows only the controls used by the selected analysis. Course comparison hides course and target; weather hides target; age comparison hides age; checkpoint comparison hides earlier performance. All ages and all recorded gender categories are genuine choices, not inferred characteristics of the visitor.

## Cold start and availability

Implemented initial profile: **All courses, all ages, all recorded genders, no earlier time, example target 4:00**. This example is defined in `lib/analysis-profile.ts` and is not an inferred visitor profile. The main explorer does not choose an arbitrary city when a cohort is missing.

The 4:00 value is an explicitly labeled example, not an inferred visitor ability or goal. Its achieved-time cohort spans 3:52:30–4:07:30, while opening, near-target, checkpoint, weather and improvement views have their own denominators. Counts come from the currently loaded result; never reuse a historical example's sample size for a new export.

The checkpoint example starts at 30 km with the 4:00 even-pace elapsed time, explicitly labeled as an example. Its two-minute band and sample are calculated from the current checkpoint shard. The visitor can replace the example; no earlier-time filter is applied.

Rank 2 starts in **All eligible finishes**, with All courses/ages/genders and a first 5 km more than 10% faster than that race's 5–20 km pace. It includes the same eligible finishes as the overall study (3,871,767 in 1652; 3,517,336 under 0934); no earlier race is needed. Its optional **Earlier-best comparison** retains the finishes with a recent benchmark (592,241 in 1652; 555,437 under 0934) with a recent benchmark and the first-10-km definition. The target-time example is not used in either mode. Both offer every recorded course and an explicit empty state when the exact cohort/opening group has fewer than 100 finishes. `comparison=all|history` selects the mode; legacy `prior=`/`previous=` links imply history only when no explicit mode is supplied. Explicit All clears prior filtering.

All courses has **no terrain profile**. Rank 7 asks the user to choose a course; it does not borrow a default course's elevation. A deliberate course selection updates the shared profile, with that change visible. The analytical ranking stays fixed while the card needs a selection.

The 1652 summary exposes 28 cities plus All courses, with exact ages for 1,291,322 of 3,871,767 eligible finishes (33.4%); its metadata records 1,337 profile cells, 1,829 opening cells, 782 near-target cells, 1,443 weather cells, 939 gains cells and 89,005 checkpoint cells. The 0934 summary (historical) exposed 29 cities plus All courses, with exact ages for 1,225,873 of 3,517,336 eligible finishes (34.9%). Exact-age coverage remains uneven, and missing age stays missing. Its metadata recorded 1,317 profile cells, 1,812 opening cells, 775 near-target cells, 1,407 weather cells, 906 gains cells and 83,597 checkpoint cells; those counts are published aggregate cells, not unique runners or guarantees that every selectable profile is available.

Read `summary.cities`, the mapped shards and each selected answer to audit current availability. Do not assume that city shard positions, supported temperature bands or example counts remain unchanged after a refresh.

These are aggregate-data availability checks, not a claim that all age/gender/prior combinations exist. For the shared personalized engine (not the new rank-2 analysis), `candidates()` broadens age/gender first while retaining prior time, then drops prior time. `checkpointResult()` also broadens recent pace trend and never changes course. Always disclose the actual relaxation; do not silently substitute a nearby target, another city or a national weather comparison. A broader comparison can be offered as an explicit user action.

## Relationship to the extended packs

The extended packs offer useful method context and additional questions. They cannot be spliced into a personalized card as if they used the same filters or denominator.

| Personalized card | Relevant extended pack | Important difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | `ext_opening_tradeoffs` | The archive uses three edition/gender/prior-band matched groups with an edition bootstrap. Rank 2 uses six descriptive groups under either a same-race or earlier-best reference; the default needs no linked history. Do not mix their opening distances, references or denominators, or call the differences adjusted effects. |
| Checkpoint | `ext_checkpoint_forecast_validation` | A separate temporal holdout forecast trained before 2024 and tested in 2024–2026; it does not validate every personalized cohort's probabilities. |
| Weather | `ext_weather_pacing_patterns` | Requires 100 finishes per edition and five editions per band; the personal weather cells require 20 per edition/group and three editions. |
| Terrain | `ext_terrain_pacing_proxy` | All-course net-grade classes are pooled descriptive proxies, without personal age/gender/history filters. |
| Courses | `ext_course_outcome_spread`, `ext_paired_course_comparisons` | The first uses broader prior-ability groups; the second compares linked course pairs and balances race order. Neither is an individual equivalent-time calculator. |
| Improvement | `ext_earlier_best_section_gains` | Related earlier-best attribution; keep its published grouping and denominators distinct from target-bucket personal cohorts. |
| Age | `ext_age_pacing` | Broader decade bands and recorded-gender comparisons, rather than the guide's five-year groups and selected achieved/prior-time cohort. |

Do not force the general forecast, paired-course or matched-opening results to obey unsupported personal controls. They can be linked under “Method and broader evidence.”

## Questions omitted from the primary ten

- **Downhill opening:** overlaps ranks 2 and 7, and the supplied-route proxy cannot establish that descent caused late slowing. Its supported descriptive result remains in the twelve-question archive after an explicit course selection.
- **Returning to the same course:** implemented and useful, but lower immediate decision value than the selected ten and restricted to linked repeats. It remains in the twelve-question archive. It is not selected by target, and its paired change cannot isolate familiarity.

The wider 35-question catalog, its 33 calculated packs, sustained-slowdown figures and compatibility URLs remain a research archive. The shared engine retains all 12 calculation paths; `summary.analyses` and metadata `analysis_count` intentionally remain 12. The full earlier guide remains at `/research/personalized`. `/your-race` forwards mapped hashes and profile parameters to the primary pages; downhill opens the new all-finisher route and return opens that archive. The ten-page selection does not remove those outputs. The earlier target-range expansion used September 7 input. The current 0934 refresh updates the source and calculation provenance, retains the same 90–720-minute target contract and adds explicitly reviewed edition exclusions without changing the pacing definitions.
