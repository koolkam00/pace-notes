# Ten analyses for Pace Notes

The ten ranked analyses at `/analyses/{slug}`: the question each answers, the data and method behind it, the controls that change it and the limit it must keep. Their methods appear on the site only on `/methodology`; each analysis links its own section (`/methodology#<id>`).

## Current source and defaults

Every analysis reads the release adopted in [`analysis/release.json`](../analysis/release.json) (rule 5 in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)). Their counts are read from the published files, never typed into pages:

- The shared personalized engine (ranks 1, 3, 4 and 7 to 10): `public/data/packs/ext_personalized_guide/summary.json` (`n`, `age_n` with an exact age, `history_n` with a recent earlier-year benchmark, `cities`) and its `pack_meta.json` (`coverage`: the published cells per path, and the release in `input_export_id`).
- Rank 2: `public/data/fast-start/all-finishers.json` and `evidence.json` ([fast-start analysis](FAST_START_ANALYSIS.md)).
- Ranks 5 and 6 and `/analyses/downhill-start`: `public/data/all-finisher-context/evidence.json` ([all-finisher analyses](ALL_FINISHER_ANALYSES.md)); rank 6's Warming and Wind tabs: `public/data/weather/evidence.json` ([weather analyses](WEATHER_ANALYSES.md)).

Under `private-export-20261021-1050` (the engine calculated 2026-10-21T16:04:43Z) the engine holds 4,108,749 eligible finishes, 1,288,687 of them with an exact age (31.4%) and 648,914 with a recent earlier-year benchmark, in 27 cities plus All courses; its published cells are 1,314 profile, 1,831 opening, 766 near-target, 1,413 weather, 933 gains and 88,968 checkpoint cells. The [1050 refresh record](REFRESH_20261021_1050.md) separates calculation, import and production. Earlier vintages are under [history](#history-earlier-releases).

Ranks 5 (courses) and 6 (weather) show all eligible finishes only: the [all-finisher context](ALL_FINISHER_ANALYSES.md) by course, age, recorded gender and 5–20 km pace band, with no prior result or target and no automatic widening. Their earlier-result comparison was cut from both pages in the redesign (October 18, 2026); the personalized `courses` and `weather` paths that computed it are still built and published, and old `comparison=history` links open the all-finisher view. Rank 9 measures improvement and so needs an earlier result; the other nine questions have a default that needs none. `/analyses/downhill-start` is a supplementary page outside the ten.

The order puts an understandable runner decision first, then the strength and availability of the evidence, then what each adds beyond the ones before it. The ranking stays fixed: changing a profile updates results and availability, never the order.

Pages show miles and minutes per mile by default, with a Miles / Kilometres switch (`units=mi|km` in a shared link; the browser keeps the choice) and elevation in feet in miles mode. The metric section names in this document describe the source measurements; on the site a recorded 5 km section reads 3.11 miles, and no individual-mile split is made. Units change displayed numbers and labels only, never the questions, the release, the cohorts, the counting thresholds or the finish durations ([display units](WEBSITE_ARCHITECTURE.md#display-units)).

## Primary order and routes

The order, titles, slugs and visible controls are defined in [lib/ten-analyses.ts](../lib/ten-analyses.ts). `/analyses` lists the ten, and the home guide and `/research` list them under their chapters (`lib/chapters.ts`). Each page opens on its answer card and offers Make it yours (`AnalysisExplorer`, or the rank's own explorer), with profile parameters shared through its links. Ranks 1, 3, 4 and 7 to 10 use the selectors in [lib/personalized.ts](../lib/personalized.ts), the fields in [lib/personalized-types.ts](../lib/personalized-types.ts) and the [personalized calculations](../analysis/build_personalized.py). Rank 2 uses the independent [fast-start analysis](FAST_START_ANALYSIS.md) (`FastStartAnalysis`), and ranks 5 and 6 the all-finisher context (`AllFinisherAnalysis`), both calculated from the same adopted runner data. The personalized opening, course and weather paths stay in the published pack; their under-target and earlier-result shares are not what ranks 2, 5 and 6 show.

## Supported controls and counting rules

The profile type has course, age, gender, target and previous time. Weather and terrain are separate data views; there is no shared age × speed × gender × elevation × weather record cube in the published personalized pack. Selecting a warm day must not appear to recalculate a cool-weather pacing profile, and selecting an uphill section must not appear to create a hill-adjusted forecast.

“Speed” has two distinct meanings and needs two labels:

- **Target finish:** a strict counting threshold for ranks 3 and 8; exact neighboring finish windows for rank 4; a centered achieved-time band for ranks 1, 7 and 9 and for rank 10 without history. Whole-minute targets run from 1:30 through 12:00, with exact counts at all 631 thresholds. The engine has 43 presets from 90 through 720 minutes; cells publish only when they meet the method’s sample requirements. Profile bands are 15 minutes wide around the nearest preset, so not every one-minute target adjustment changes those profiles.
- **Earlier recorded best:** an optional 15-minute band of the fastest eligible result in the two strictly earlier calendar years. It is not an exact last marathon time. It changes supported cohorts, especially ranks 5 and 6, but never the checkpoint comparison. Do not silently fill it from the visitor's target. Rank 2's optional history mode instead uses four explicit earlier-best bands (under 3:00, 3:00–3:29:59, 3:30–3:59:59, and 4:00 or longer), plus All. Its default All eligible finishes mode has no earlier-time or speed filter. Both modes use exact cohorts without automatic widening.

Weather supports the existing bands below 10°C, 10–14.9°C, 15–19.9°C and 20°C or warmer only where cells exist. Available bands are determined by the current aggregate and selected cohort. The chart displays those observed bands; it does not generate intermediate temperatures or filter the other nine analyses. At least two bands are needed to claim a cooler/warmer comparison. A one-band result is labeled as coverage, not a cooler/warmer contrast.

Terrain supports a supplied profile for an explicitly selected course. The chart switcher displays net change, supplied gain/loss when available, and the corresponding observed pace profile. There is no elevation slider that generates new runner cohorts, corrected paces or a causal hill adjustment. A separate all-course terrain-class chart already exists in `ext_terrain_pacing_proxy`, but its classes mix different courses and are not the personalized cohort.

The explorer shows only the controls used by the selected analysis. Course comparison hides course and target; weather hides target; age comparison hides age; checkpoint comparison hides earlier performance. All ages and all recorded gender categories are genuine choices, not inferred characteristics of the visitor.

## Cold start and availability

Implemented initial profile: **All courses, all ages, all recorded genders, no earlier time, example target 4:00**. This example is defined in `lib/analysis-profile.ts` and is not an inferred visitor profile. The main explorer does not choose an arbitrary city when a cohort is missing.

The 4:00 value is an explicitly labeled example, not an inferred visitor ability or goal. Its achieved-time cohort spans 3:52:30–4:07:30, while opening, near-target, checkpoint, weather and improvement views have their own denominators. Counts come from the currently loaded result; never reuse a historical example's sample size for a new export.

The checkpoint example starts at 30 km with the 4:00 even-pace elapsed time, explicitly labeled as an example. Its two-minute band and sample are calculated from the current checkpoint shard. The visitor can replace the example; no earlier-time filter is applied.

Rank 2 starts in **All eligible finishes**, with All courses/ages/genders and a first 5 km more than 10% faster than that race's 5–20 km pace. It includes the same eligible finishes as the overall study (`analysis_n` in `fast-start/all-finishers.json`: 4,108,749 under 1050); no earlier race is needed. Its optional **Earlier-best comparison** keeps the finishes with a recent benchmark (`history_n`: 648,914 under 1050) and uses the first-10-km definition. The target-time example is not used in either mode. Both offer every recorded course and an explicit empty state when the exact cohort/opening group has fewer than 100 finishes. `comparison=all|history` selects the mode; legacy `prior=`/`previous=` links imply history only when no explicit mode is supplied. Explicit All clears prior filtering.

All courses has **no terrain profile**. Rank 7 asks the user to choose a course; it does not borrow a default course's elevation. A deliberate course selection updates the shared profile, with that change visible. The analytical ranking stays fixed while the card needs a selection.

The current counts are under [current source and defaults](#current-source-and-defaults). Exact-age coverage is uneven, and a missing age stays missing. The cell counts in `pack_meta.json` are published aggregate cells, not unique runners, and not a promise that every selectable profile is available.

Read `summary.cities`, the mapped shards and each selected answer to audit current availability. Do not assume that city shard positions, supported temperature bands or example counts remain unchanged after a refresh.

These are aggregate-data availability checks, not a claim that all age/gender/prior combinations exist. For the shared personalized engine (not the new rank-2 analysis), `candidates()` broadens age/gender first while retaining prior time, then drops prior time. `checkpointResult()` also broadens recent pace trend and never changes course. Always disclose the actual relaxation; do not silently substitute a nearby target, another city or a national weather comparison. A broader comparison can be offered as an explicit user action.

- **Downhill opening:** overlaps ranks 2 and 7, and the supplied-route proxy cannot establish that descent caused late slowing. Its all-finisher view is the supplementary page `/analyses/downhill-start`.
- **Returning to the same course:** implemented and useful, but lower immediate decision value than the selected ten and restricted to linked repeats. Its path stays in the personalized pack (`return`), with no page of its own. It is not selected by target, and its paired change cannot isolate familiarity.

The target range stays 90 to 720 minutes, and each release's reviewed edition exclusions change no pacing definition.

## Product and statistical contracts

These came from the project handoff on October 30, 2026; its dated original is in [history](history/handoff-2026-09-to-10-13.md#product-and-statistical-contracts).

The archive retains 35 broader questions: 33 calculated extension packs and two limited by missing group/start-offset measurements. These overlapping views are not independent studies.

The sustained-slowdown calculation uses contiguous recorded sections totaling at least 5 km after 20 km; onset is a section boundary, not an exact moment. The definition remains at least 25% slowing for at least 5 km after 20 km relative to the 5–20 km baseline, with the neutral [published-method citation](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251513). Canonical record IDs are not verified cross-race identities: retain ambiguity, gender/birth-year and duplicate-edition checks, and exclude current/same-year results from prior benchmarks.

The defaults (the initial profile, the target range and the earlier-time bands) are under [cold start and availability](#cold-start-and-availability) and [supported controls and counting rules](#supported-controls-and-counting-rules); units are under [display units](WEBSITE_ARCHITECTURE.md#display-units).
